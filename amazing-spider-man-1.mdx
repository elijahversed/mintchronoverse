[**Amazing Fantasy** **#15**](/comics/silver-age/amazing-fantasy-15) had been a one-off gamble. This issue is the payoff — Marvel greenlighting an ongoing series on the strength of reader response alone, which was unusual enough at the time to say something about how the character had landed.

## What happens

Peter Parker is now a young college student, but his social life hasn't improved much. His classmates see him as a bookworm, and although Peter wants to fit in, he remains largely isolated from the other students. He continues to excel academically, particularly in chemistry, while living with his Aunt May and Uncle Ben.

Peter's life had changed dramatically after being bitten by a radioactive spider. The bite gave him spider-like abilities, including superhuman strength, agility, and the ability to cling to walls and buildings.

After discovering his powers, Peter developed his own web-shooters and other equipment, creating the identity of Spider-Man. At first, however, he primarily used his abilities to pursue fame and money.

The death of Uncle Ben had changed that outlook. After allowing a fleeing robber to escape, Peter later discovered that the same criminal had killed Ben. The experience taught Peter that having the ability to help someone also meant having a responsibility to do so.

Now Peter attempts to balance his life as a college student with his growing career as Spider-Man.

### The Daily Bugle

Peter begins performing public stunts as Spider-Man, earning money while using his abilities to support himself and Aunt May.

His growing public profile, however, attracts the attention of **J. Jonah Jameson**, publisher of the _Daily Bugle_. Jameson becomes convinced that Spider-Man is a dangerous menace and uses his newspaper to turn public opinion against him.

<Tip>
  Jameson's antagonism toward Spider-Man — publishing him as a menace rather than a hero — starts here and becomes one of the series' longest-running throughlines.
</Tip>

Peter eventually visits the _Daily Bugle_ and discovers that Jameson's son, John Jameson, is preparing to travel into space aboard a rocket.

During the launch, the rocket suffers a mechanical failure and begins falling back toward Earth. Spider-Man intervenes and manages to rescue John Jameson.

Peter expects his actions to finally convince Jameson that Spider-Man isn't the menace he claims him to be.

Instead, Jameson turns the situation against him.

Rather than thanking Spider-Man for saving his son's life, Jameson accuses him of being responsible for the disaster in the first place.

Peter's attempt to improve his reputation has only made Jameson's campaign against Spider-Man worse.

### The Chameleon

Determined to clear his name, Spider-Man follows a lead that brings him to a rooftop, where he has been summoned by a mysterious figure.

Waiting for him is **the Chameleon**, a master of disguise who has begun using Spider-Man's reputation and identity as part of his own plans.

Their encounter marks the beginning of Spider-Man's first major conflict with a costumed supervillain and establishes an important pattern that will continue throughout Peter's life: even when Spider-Man does the right thing, the world doesn't always see him as a hero.

## Why it matters

_The Amazing Spider-Man #1_ takes the foundation established in _Amazing Fantasy #15_ and turns it into an ongoing superhero series.

Peter is no longer simply discovering what it means to be Spider-Man. He now has to maintain that identity while dealing with college, money, family, public opinion, and increasingly dangerous enemies.

The issue also establishes several elements that become central to Spider-Man's mythology, including his complicated relationship with the _Daily Bugle_, J. Jonah Jameson's hostility toward him, and his first confrontation with the Chameleon.

Most importantly, Peter's decision to continue using his powers for good despite receiving little recognition reinforces one of the defining tensions of the character: **Spider-Man does what is right even when doing so makes his own life harder.**
