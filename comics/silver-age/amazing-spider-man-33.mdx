The final chapter of a three-issue arc, this issue closes out the "Master Planner" storyline with a sequence that's been referenced, homaged, and reproduced in nearly every subsequent adaptation of the character in some form.

## What happens

Trapped and pinned beneath heavy wreckage in a flooding underwater base, with the antidote his dying Aunt May needs sitting just out of reach, Peter is left with no external help, no convenient rescue, and no shortcut — only whether he's willing to keep trying after every physical indication says he can't.

<Note>
  This is Ditko's artwork doing most of the storytelling — several pages carry almost no dialogue, relying entirely on Peter's internal narration and expression to build the moment.
</Note>

## Why it matters

Earlier issues had established Peter as physically powerful but emotionally and financially strained. This issue is where that combination pays off dramatically: his strength was never really the point, his refusal to quit was. It's frequently cited by writers and artists as the single issue that best captures why the character resonated the way he did, and it's a direct ancestor of very similar "won't stop, can't stop" beats that show up in later comics, films, and games throughout this archive.
