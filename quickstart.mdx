**You don't need to read this to use Chronoverse — but if you've got two minutes, it'll save you some backtracking.**

## The Four Categories

Everything in the archive sits in one of four places, accessible from the tabs at the top of the page.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Comics">
    The print history. Organized roughly chronologically, from _Amazing Fantasy_ #15 through the major eras and event storylines that followed.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Games">
    Interactive appearances — arcade titles, licensed console games, and the modern open-world entries.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Films">
    Live-action and animated features, grouped by trilogy or era so you can see how each version of the story built on (or ignored) the one before it.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Characters">
    Profiles for the people who recur across all three media above — heroes, villains, love interests, and the occasional universe-hopping variant.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## How entries are organized

Within each category, pages are grouped — for example, the Films category groups movies by trilogy (Raimi, Webb, MCU) rather than dumping every film into one flat list. Some entries also nest under a parent page, usually when something is a direct expansion of the page above it rather than a standalone topic.

<Tip>
  If you're trying to compare two versions of the same story beat — say, Uncle Ben's death across different films — start from the **Characters** entry for the character in question. Character pages cross-link to every appearance, which is usually faster than hunting through each category separately.
</Tip>

## Reading an entry page

Every entry follows the same shape, so once you've read one, you've read the format:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Header">
    Title, one-line summary, and tags — quick signals for what era, medium, or continuity the entry belongs to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Body">
    The write-up itself: background, key details, and why the entry matters in the broader Spider-Man story.
  </Step>
  <Step title="On this page">
    A jump-to-section outline on the right, for longer entries with multiple headings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Previous / Next">
    Footer navigation to the adjacent entry in the same group — handy for reading a trilogy or run start to finish.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## A note on completeness

This archive grows page by page rather than trying to launch "finished." If something you'd expect to find isn't here yet, that's a gap to fill in later, not an oversight worth worrying about — check back, or see the [FAQ](/faq) for how the archive gets updated.
