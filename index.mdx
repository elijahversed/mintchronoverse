Spider-Man has been rebooted, remastered, reimagined, and re-drawn more times than almost any character in fiction. <Tooltip tip="This website!">Chronoverse</Tooltip> exists to keep track of all of it — one archive, organized, cross-referenced, and built for browsing rather than scrolling through a wiki with forty tabs open.

<Note>
  This is a fan-made reference project. It isn't affiliated with Marvel, Sony, or Insomniac Games — just an attempt to organize a sprawling, decades-deep character history into something pleasant to explore.
</Note>

## Why this exists

Spider-Man doesn't have one continuity — he has dozens, loosely stitched together by the same handful of ideas: a kid gets powers, a loved one dies because of an unavoidable plot point and the guilt turns into a promise. Everything else — the villains, the girlfriends, the universes, the suits — gets rebuilt around that promise every few years by a new writer, director, or studio.

 

That's fascinating, but it's also genuinely hard to keep straight. Chronoverse is an attempt to make the differences \*legible\* — what changed between Raimi's trilogy and the MCU version, what Miles Morales owes to the Ultimate universe, which game canon a given costume actually belongs to.
